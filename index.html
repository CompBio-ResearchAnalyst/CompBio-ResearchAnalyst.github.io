Featured research - independent, solo-authored

Mapping the immunosuppressive trajectory of glioblastoma myeloid cells

Read the preprint ↗
11patients, glioblastoma cohort
101,028cells, 4-method cross-validated atlas
43,918cells, myeloid trajectory sub-atlas
264TCGA-GBM patients, clinical validation
  • 101,028 cells annotated across four independent methods, cross-validated against dual-algorithm copy-number inference
  • A single convergent route to a terminally immunosuppressive TREM2⁺ state, unfolding in two kinetically distinct phases
  • RUNX1 identified as the most robust regulator among seven candidates - held up against sequencing-depth and single-patient confounding, linked to TGF-β/SMAD signalling
  • Validated spatially (RCTD deconvolution, spatial autocorrelation) and clinically (264 TCGA-GBM patients) - including a null result, reported as found

Code and pipeline are not public for this project - the manuscript and its figures are the intended record of the work.
